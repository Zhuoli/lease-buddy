RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — FICTIONAL)

1. PARTIES. This Lease is between Example Property Management LLC ("Landlord") and the undersigned resident ("Tenant") for Unit 4B at Maple Court Apartments.

2. TERM. The term of this Lease begins on November 1, 2026 and ends on October 31, 2027.

3. RENT. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $2,150 per month, due on the 1st day of each month, without demand or deduction.

4. LATE CHARGES. If rent is not received by the 5th day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $150, plus $15 per day until rent is paid in full.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,150. A cleaning fee of $300 is non-refundable and will be deducted from the deposit at move-out regardless of the condition of the unit.

6. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for electricity, internet, and a monthly utility administration fee of $25. Landlord pays water and trash.

7. USE AND OCCUPANCY. The unit shall be used only as a private residence for the Tenant listed above. Guests staying more than fourteen (14) consecutive days require Landlord's written approval.

8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the unit clean and is responsible for the cost of any repair under $250, regardless of cause. Tenant shall report water leaks within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery.

9. LANDLORD ENTRY. Landlord may enter the unit at any time upon reasonable notice to inspect, make repairs, or show the unit to prospective tenants.

10. PETS. No pets are allowed without Landlord's prior written consent. Approved pets require pet rent of $50 per month and a non-refundable pet fee of $400.

11. SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not sublet the unit or assign this Lease without Landlord's prior written consent.

12. EARLY TERMINATION. Tenant may end this Lease before the end of the term by giving sixty (60) days' written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to two (2) months' rent.

13. RENEWAL. This Lease shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

14. NOTICE TO VACATE. Tenant shall give Landlord at least thirty (30) days' written notice before vacating at the end of the term.

15. RENT INCREASES. Landlord may increase rent for any renewal term by giving Tenant written notice at least thirty (30) days before the renewal term begins.

16. ATTORNEY FEES. If Landlord takes legal action to enforce this Lease, Tenant shall pay Landlord's reasonable attorney fees and court costs.

ADDENDUM A — COMMUNITY RULES

A1. QUIET HOURS. Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM daily.

A2. ENTRY. Management will provide at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice before entering a unit, except in an emergency.

A3. RENT PAYMENT. Rent received after the 3rd day of the month is late and subject to late charges.
